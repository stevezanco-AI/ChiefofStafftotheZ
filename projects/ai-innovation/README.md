# AI Innovation Initiative — Southwest Inside Sales Team

**Owner:** Steve Zanco, Inside Sales Manager — Southwest
**Status:** In planning
**Strategic purpose:** Build AI-powered tools that elevate team performance, customer experience, and operational intelligence — while positioning Steve as an AI-forward leader operating at Director/Senior Manager scope.

**Why this matters beyond the team:**
This initiative demonstrates exactly what Zack and Phil described as needed for the Senior Manager of Sales Operations path: larger-scope thinking, cross-functional impact, and innovation that didn't exist before Steve championed it.

## The Four Tools

| Tool | Purpose | Primary Beneficiary | Visibility Impact |
|------|---------|--------------------|--------------------|
| [1. Team Chief of Staff](./01-team-chief-of-staff.md) | Personalized AI development partner for each of Steve's 19 reps | Team engagement, development, Gallup scores | High — scalable people leadership |
| [2. Dreamweaver](./02-dreamweaver.md) | Unreasonable Hospitality idea generator for customer moments | Reps, customers, NPS | High — customer experience innovation |
| [3. Strategic Accounts Dashboard](./03-strategic-accounts-dashboard.md) | AI-powered account health and action intelligence | Steve, team, leadership | Very High — exec visibility tool |
| [4. Customer Portal Utilization](./04-customer-portal-utilization.md) | AI-driven adoption plan and training system | Customers, reps, operations | Medium-High — ties to key metric |
