# Framework: Team Capability Matrix

Use when making decisions about: promotions, performance management, hiring, team structure.

## The Matrix

Rate each team member on two axes:

**Performance** (1-5): Are they hitting their number and delivering results?
**Potential** (1-5): How much more can they grow? Are they coachable?

| | Low Potential (1-2) | Medium Potential (3) | High Potential (4-5) |
|-|---------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **High Performer (4-5)** | Sustain — they're valuable, limited ceiling | Develop — might surprise you | Invest heavily — these are your stars |
| **Mid Performer (3)** | Manage closely — clarify expectations | Coach — what's blocking them? | Unlock — find the missing ingredient |
| **Low Performer (1-2)** | Exit plan — don't delay | Urgent coaching — 30/60/90 day plan | Situational — wrong role? wrong manager? |

## Promotion Decision Guide

Before promoting anyone, answer:
1. Have they already been doing the next job's work?
2. Do they make people around them better?
3. Will they stretch into the role or coast in it?
4. What's the cost of NOT promoting (retention risk)?
5. Is there a ready backfill for their current role?

**Rule:** Promote on demonstrated performance at the next level, not potential alone.

## Hire vs. Promote Decision

| Factor | Favor Internal | Favor External |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| Culture fit | Strong | Weak |
| Skills gap | Closeable | Requires new DNA |
| Speed to productivity | Faster | Slower |
| Morale signal | Promotes loyalty | May signal ceiling |
| Fresh perspective needed | No | Yes |
