# Framework: Stakeholder Communication

Use when preparing to communicate upward (directors, VPs, C-suite) or laterally (peers, cross-functional partners).

## The BLOT Structure (Bottom Line On Top)

1. **Bottom line first:** State the conclusion or ask in the first sentence
2. **Evidence:** 2-3 supporting data points or facts
3. **Implication:** What this means for the business
4. **Ask:** What you need from them (decision, resource, air cover)

## Audience Calibration

| Audience | What They Care About | Language to Use | Length |
|----------|---------------------|-----------------|--------|
| CEO / SVP | Revenue, risk, market position | Business impact, % and $ | < 3 minutes |
| VP Sales | Pipeline, attainment, team health | Numbers, trends | 5 minutes |
| Peer managers | Collaboration, shared goals | We / us framing | Conversational |
| HR / Ops | Process, compliance, headcount | Policy alignment | Structured |

## Common Scenarios

### Asking for a promotion / title change
- Lead with business impact of your contributions
- Frame it as what the company needs, not what you want
- Have a number in mind before the conversation

### Delivering bad news
- Lead with the problem, not the backstory
- Follow with: what happened, what you're doing, what you need
- Never let them hear it from someone else first

### Presenting results
- Headline: are you on track or not?
- Then: why, and what's next
- Don't bury the miss in good news
