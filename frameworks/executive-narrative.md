# Framework: Executive Narrative

Use when crafting talking points, presentations, or stories for executive audiences.

## The Narrative Arc

1. **The situation:** Where are we and why does it matter now?
2. **The complication:** What's the problem, tension, or opportunity?
3. **The resolution:** What are we doing about it and why this approach?
4. **The ask / next step:** What needs to happen next?

## Talking Points Template

```
[HEADLINE — One punchy sentence that captures the whole story]

Context: [1 sentence — why we're here]
The number: [Most important metric — with comparison to benchmark]
The insight: [What the number tells us]
What we're doing: [Action in plain language]
What we need: [Specific ask]
```

## Punchy Language Rules

- Verbs over nouns: "We closed" not "Closure was achieved"
- Numbers first: "42% growth" not "Growth of 42%"
- Short sentences under pressure: If you're nervous, short sentences won't betray you
- Avoid: "leverage," "synergy," "moving the needle," "circle back"

## Voice Calibration for Steve Zanco

- Direct, confident, data-backed
- Competitive without being arrogant
- Ties results back to team — you win together
- Acknowledges reality; doesn't spin
