## Financial Philosophy

Steve's approach to money should reflect his values: accountability, intentionality, and growth. Money is a tool — for security, for options, for generosity, for the life he's building.

**Core principles:**
- Know the numbers. Not tracking is not neutral — it's drift.
- Build optionality. Financial security changes how you negotiate, what risks you take, and how you handle career decisions.
- Income growth is a strategy, not an accident. Promotions, consulting, and smart investing are all part of the plan.
- Avoid lifestyle inflation that traps you. The goal is freedom, not just a bigger number.

## Annual Financial Review

Do this in December or January. Takes 1-2 hours. Worth it.

**Annual review questions:**
1. What was my total compensation this year? How does it compare to my market value?
2. What is my net worth today vs. one year ago? What drove the change?
3. Am I on track for my 5-year goals?
4. What financial decision from this year am I most glad I made?
5. What financial decision do I wish I had made differently?
6. What does my financial picture look like if I get the Director promotion? If I don't?
7. Is my financial strategy still aligned with where I want to be in 5 years?
