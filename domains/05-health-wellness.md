**Domain tag:** `health-wellness`

---

## The Core Truth

Steve's output depends entirely on his input. The most strategic, well-connected, high-performing version of Steve only exists when he's physically rested, mentally sharp, and emotionally regulated. Neglecting this domain doesn't just hurt Steve — it degrades performance in every other domain.

This isn't about being a health fanatic. It's about protecting the asset. Steve is the asset.

---

## Energy Management Philosophy

**Energy is the real currency, not time.** Steve could have 16 free hours in a day. If he's running on 4 hours of sleep and no exercise, those 16 hours are low-quality hours. The goal is not more time — it's more high-quality time.

**Energy comes from four sources:**
1. **Physical:** Sleep, exercise, nutrition, recovery
2. **Mental:** Focused work, clear priorities, not multitasking, strategic breaks
3. **Emotional:** Relationships that restore, meaning in the work, processing stress
4. **Spiritual/Purpose:** Work that matters, alignment between values and action

When energy drops, diagnose which source is depleted before trying to push through.

## Warning Signs

These are the signals that Steve is depleting, not sustaining. When two or more appear simultaneously, something needs to change.

- **Sleep drops below 6 hours** for more than two nights in a row
- **Exercise skipped** for more than a week
- **Irritability** that family or close colleagues notice
- **"I'll just push through"** becoming the default answer to everything
- **Loss of enthusiasm** for things that normally energize him
- **Numbing behaviors increasing** (excessive scrolling, extra drinks, zoning out)
- **Decision fatigue** — struggling to make simple choices
- **Everything feels urgent** — loss of ability to prioritize

When warning signs appear: the Chief of Staff will flag them if they appear in /debrief data. Steve should feel no shame about it — it's data, not judgment.
