## Team Management

### Weekly / Daily Rituals

- **Weekly:** Team updates, progress on Gallup action items, project syncs
- **Daily:** Team check-ins, follow-through on open actions

### Hiring & Onboarding

- Just hired 2 new team members — current focus is structured onboarding
- Current gap: keeping the team motivated to develop; no structured new-hire onboarding process yet
- Coaching priority: build a coaching cadence that develops leaders-in-training and raises frontline performance

### Coaching Cadence

**Weekly:**
- Individual 1:1s with each rep (30 min minimum)
  - First 10 min: rep owns the agenda — what do they need?
  - Next 15 min: deal review or skill coaching on something specific
  - Last 5 min: commitment — what will they do differently this week?
- Team pipeline review (60 min)
  - Every deal above [threshold] gets a line item
  - Call out stale deals — don't let them age unaddressed
  - Celebrate specific wins publicly

**Monthly:**
- Formal performance check-in (30-45 min)
  - Quota pacing review
  - Skill development progress
  - Career conversation — where are they going?
  - Written summary in rep's file

**Quarterly:**
- Full performance review against quarterly goals
- Update development plans
- Calibration with HR/Director on performance tier (top/core/at-risk)

### Rep Development Framework

For each rep, maintain clarity on:
1. **Current performance tier:** High performer / Core performer / Needs improvement
2. **Strongest skill:** What they do better than anyone on the team
3. **Development focus:** The ONE thing that would most move their number
4. **Career trajectory:** Where do they want to go? Is the current role a step toward it?
5. **Retention risk:** Green / Yellow / Red — and what would make them leave?

### Performance Management

When a rep is underperforming:
- First: diagnose before prescribing. Is it skill or will?
- Skill problem: coaching plan with specific behaviors, weekly check-in
- Will problem: more direct conversation — is this role the right fit?
- Formal PIP: only after coaching hasn't worked and documentation is in place
- Never let underperformance fester beyond 60 days without a clear plan
